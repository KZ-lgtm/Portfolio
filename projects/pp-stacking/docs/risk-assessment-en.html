1 · Machine limits (ISO 12100 §5.3)

Equipment: ABB IRB 4600-40/2.55 (kinematics and joint limits from its RobotStudio PIM.xml) floor-mounted at the origin, front to −X; fork gripper ≈ 22 kg bolted to the A6 flange, flange facing sideways along the tines — robot axis 6 flips the sheet (crossbar 1400 mm, 4 fixed tines + 4 pneumatic clamp fingers 1230 mm, stripper bar); pick table (rollers with fork slots, servo end stop) at x −2.9 → −1.6 / y 0.55 → 2.45 m, roller top 0.95 m; infeed buffer belt through the top fence (hood + 80 mm slot); run-out belt with manual off-load outside; 2-roller embossing (4), traction and flying cutter (5) from the line supplier; 6-station chain conveyor at y −2.3 m (top 0.40 m, 0.3 m/s): 1 pallet stacker (10 pallets) · 2 empty · 3 stacking (x −1.3) · 4–6 full-pallet buffer; stations 2–4 in a fenced tunnel; AGV / forklift lanes at both conveyor ends; cabinets outside the fence.
Product: PP flooring sheets 1000 wide × 1000 / 1300 / 1500 long × 3 mm (≈ 3.9–5.9 kg at 1.3 t/m³), decor face up from the line; stacked decor face to decor face (every 2nd sheet flipped), 200 sheets per pallet (adjustable 2–400) → 600 mm, ≤ 1.2 t; pallets 1600 × 1100 with slip sheet.
Rates and speeds: line 6 sheets / min (web 6–9 m/min); robot TCP 2500 mm/s (SafeMove Tool Speed Supervision), 250 mm/s in the 60 mm approach zones, tine withdrawal 1500 mm/s; flip 180° by A6 in 0.9 s; belts 1.2 m/s; pallet conveyor 0.3 m/s; measured robot cycle 4.4–5.7 s, capacity 7.3 sheets / min.
Space limits: fence x −3.5 → 1.2 m, y −1.5 → 3.2 m, H2000, plus the pallet tunnel x −4.85 → 2.0 m, y −3.3 → −1.5 m; openings: infeed belt (top, x −2.95 → −1.5 m, hood), door (right, y 0.6 → 1.8 m), tunnel curtains (x −4.85 and 2.0 m), stacker loading side (x −7.15 m).
Use: automatic operation with operators outside (HMI, manual off-load, stacker refill by forklift, shipping by AGV / forklift); intervention through the guard-locked door; maintenance under LOTO. The upstream extrusion / calender (1–3) is not part of this assessment.
