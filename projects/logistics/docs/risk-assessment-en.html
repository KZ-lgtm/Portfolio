1 · Machine limits (ISO 12100 §5.3)

Equipment: ABB IRB 6700-150/3.20 (R1, 0.4 m pedestal, L-fork 0.3 m under the flange, bin centre 0.55 m ahead) in a cylinder shelf of 26 columns × 8 levels (inner face r 2.0 m, 144 slots, ports east / west, passage north, frame 3.75 m) · 2 × IRB 1300-12/1.4 (R2 / R3, 4-cup vacuum gripper, 3D camera over each bin stop) · 2 × IRB 930-22/1.05 (S1 / S2 for the IRB 360 of the video, 1.05 m pedestals, bellows cup) — kinematics and joint limits from the RobotStudio PIM.xml; zone conveyors (picking and restock loops with RATs), item belt with bar-code arch, 8 powered put-wall lanes.
Product and orders (assumed — the video shows neither): 40 SKUs in cartons S 120 × 90 × 60 / M 160 × 120 × 80 / L 240 × 170 × 110 mm (≤ 1.6 kg), 3 bins per SKU (8–32 cartons per bin), orders of 1–4 lines × 1–3 pcs (Pareto or flat demand), up to 8 active orders (one per put-wall lane, rolling waves).
Rates (digital layout, 4 × 1 h): 677 items / h and 205 orders / h with Pareto demand (2.0 items per bin presentation, R1 88 % busy — the shelf robot is the bottleneck), 655 / 194 flat demand, 769 items / 144 orders per hour with 6-line orders, 5 % no-read and 90 s restock, 245 single-line orders / h at 70 % robot speed (packing operator 93 % busy).
Space limits: fence x −3.2 → 10.6 m, y −3.2 → 3.45 m, H2000; guard-locked doors north (x 0.5 → 1.4, in line with the cylinder passage) and south (x 4.6 → 5.5); restock tunnel west (curtain at x −4.25), put-wall tunnels north (curtains at y 4.3), exception chute east; cabinets MCP, RC1, SC1 south and RC2 north; walkways outside.
Use: automatic operation with two operators outside (restock at the west stop ≈ 20 bins / h; packing at the put wall + exception tote 77–93 % busy); interventions through the doors with the cell stopped; ring entries under SF3 / SF4; maintenance under LOTO.

3 · Risk estimation method (ISO/TR 14121-2 matrix)

Severity ↓ / Probability →Very likelyLikelyUnlikelyRemote
Catastrophic — Death or permanent disabilityHighHighHighMedium
Serious — Irreversible injury (amputation, fracture with lasting damage)HighHighMediumLow
Moderate — Reversible injury requiring medical treatmentHighMediumLowNegligible
Minor — First-aid injuryMediumLowNegligibleNegligible
Probability combines exposure, probability of the hazardous event and possibility of avoidance (ISO 12100 §5.5.2). Initial risk is estimated with no safeguards; residual risk after the listed measures.
Acceptance: High — Not acceptable — further risk reduction mandatory · Medium — Tolerable only with the listed measures implemented, verified and documented (ALARP) · Low — Acceptable · Negligible — Acceptable, no action
PLr from the ISO 13849-1 risk graph (S · F · P); ISO 10218-2 sets PL d, Category 3 as the default minimum for robot-cell safety functions.
